Create a Telegram Bot with BotFather
BotFather is Telegram's official bot for creating new bots. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather.
✈️ Open BotFather
In BotFather, type /newbot → give it a name (e.g. "Mama's Kitchen") → give it a username ending in "bot" (e.g. "mamaskitchenph_bot") → BotFather gives you a TOKEN — copy it, you need it in Step 3.
2
Enter your Telegram Bot Token
Paste the token BotFather gave you here. It looks like: 7412345678:AAFxxxx...
⚠️ Keep this token private — never share it publicly. It gives full control of your bot.
